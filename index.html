Featured Project: Charity Gala Live Production

The 'A Soirée of Giving — Weaving Hong Kong Together' charity gala was held in the Vista Ballroom at The American Club. The photo shows my front-of-house (FOH) position that night; the videos show the ballroom lighting and screens in action.

One desk runs the whole show: video, lighting and audio in sync

FOH position — video switching, lighting & show calling in one desk

The position handled three video outputs (main screen, side screens and preview), playback of the event key visuals, the lighting console and tablet-controlled audio levels, following the full run sheet cue by cue through the programme.

Ballroom lighting was programmed before the dinner: purple themed wall washes, stage accents and screen content working together, so the atmosphere was set the moment guests took their seats.
